Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This is a private, self-hosted instance of open-source social media scheduling software, operated by an individual for personal, non-commercial use. It is used only by the operator and explicitly authorized accounts.

1. Who This Applies To

This policy covers the operator's own use of the service. The service is not open to public sign-up and does not knowingly process data of any third party other than the operator's own connected accounts.

2. Information We Process

To function, the service stores: the operator's account login details, authentication tokens issued by connected social media platforms (e.g. TikTok, Meta/Instagram), and the content the operator chooses to schedule or upload. This data is stored solely on the operator's own self-hosted infrastructure.

3. How Information Is Used

Data is used exclusively to authenticate with connected platforms and to schedule, upload, or publish the operator's own content to those platforms at the operator's direction. It is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

4. Third-Party Platforms

When content is published to a connected platform, that platform's own privacy policy governs how it handles the content. Access tokens can be revoked at any time from the connected platform's settings.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data persists only on the operator's infrastructure and is deleted when the operator removes the connected account or decommissions the service. To request deletion, the operator simply revokes access and removes the stored data.

6. Security

The service runs on private infrastructure behind authenticated access controls. No analytics, tracking, or third-party data collection is performed by this instance.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator at the email associated with this domain's registration.
