## Decisions Made

**Inline editing for reptile alerts:** Used Radix Collapsible with expand-one-at-a-time pattern for clean UX. Users click to expand, edit settings, save or cancel.

**Age-aware defaults:** Calculate age category (hatchling < 6mo, juvenile < 18mo, adult) client-side and show recommended thresholds. Hatchlings get higher gain thresholds (25%) since they grow rapidly.

**URL param pre-selection:** ReptileAlertsTab supports `?reptile={id}` to pre-expand a specific reptile. Enables deep linking from other parts of the app.

**Template reuse:** TemplatesTab is a simple wrapper around existing NotificationTemplatesTab. No duplication, just integration.

**Grouped schedules:** ScheduleNotificationsTab groups schedules by reptile (collapsible), with type filter to narrow down. Hides reptiles with no matching schedules.
